FlickOff @VERSION@

Keep your Mac. Lose the flicker.

Set Touch Bar brightness separately from your screen, or keep its display off.

  • Installs FlickOff in Applications.
  • Updates an existing installation and keeps your settings.
  • Closes a running copy safely before replacing it.
  • Starts FlickOff after installation in your user session.

Requires an Apple silicon MacBook Pro with a Touch Bar and macOS 13 or later. Tested on the M1 13-inch model with macOS 27.2. This is a public beta.

Free for noncommercial use. Commercial use requires a separate paid license. FlickOff is a software workaround and cannot repair a panel.
