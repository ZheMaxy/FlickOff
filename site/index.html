Touch Bar flickering? Hold it steady, or turn it off.

FlickOff is a menu bar app for Apple silicon MacBook Pro. It gives the Touch Bar the brightness control macOS doesn't have, so it can stay out of the dim range where it flickers, flashes or blinks.

Download for Mac Or install with Homebrew

Public beta · Apple silicon · macOS 13 or later · Free for noncommercial use

What it does

Set the brightness yourself

Choose a level for the Touch Bar, separately from the screen. It stays there instead of following the light in the room.

Skip the dim range

Instead of dimming when you stop using the Mac, it can turn off after 1, 5 or 15 minutes, and light again when you come back.

Or keep it dark

Off keeps the display dark while the app runs. Touch ID and touch input keep working.

Why a Touch Bar needs this

You're working in a dim room. The Touch Bar gets darker, and starts flashing. macOS gives you no separate brightness slider for it, and the brightness controls on the Touch Bar adjust the main screen, not the strip that's flickering.

FlickOff started with the author's M1 MacBook Pro, whose Touch Bar flickers at low brightness while a higher fixed level helps. If you want to work out what's going on with yours first, see what to try when a Touch Bar flickers.

This is a workaround, not a repair. It can't fix a failing panel and won't solve every kind of flicker. It uses private Apple frameworks, so a macOS update can break it; if that happens it shows a report and leaves the panel alone.

Install

With the installer

  1. Download the disk image, open it, and open Install FlickOff.
  2. This beta isn't notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway and confirm Open. You don't need an Apple Developer account. Full first-launch steps.
  3. Choose Agree and Install and confirm the administrator prompt. FlickOff appears in the menu bar.

With Homebrew

No first-launch steps and no administrator password. What this means.

brew tap zhemaxy/flickoff https://github.com/ZheMaxy/FlickOff
brew install --cask zhemaxy/flickoff/flickoff

Questions

Can FlickOff stop my Touch Bar from flickering?

It can if your Touch Bar only flickers at low brightness, which is how it started on the author's M1 MacBook Pro: FlickOff keeps it at a higher, fixed level, and can turn it off when idle instead of letting it dim. It can't repair a failing panel and won't solve every kind of flicker.

Does FlickOff work on Intel MacBook Pro models?

No. FlickOff is for Apple silicon only. It has been tested on the 13-inch M1 MacBook Pro (2020) with macOS 27.2. The 13-inch M2 model may work too, but hasn't been verified.

Does turning off the Touch Bar disable Touch ID?

No. FlickOff's Off mode keeps the display dark while the app runs. Touch ID keeps working, and so does touch input, so the invisible keys still respond.
