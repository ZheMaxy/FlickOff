FlickOff is installed.

FlickOff starts automatically in your user session.

  1. Look for FlickOff in the menu bar. It does not show a Dock icon.
  2. Choose Manual and a comfortable stable brightness, or Off.

If the icon is not visible, press Command–Space, type FlickOff, and press Return.

Your previous settings are kept. Future updates are available through Check for Updates… in the app menu.
