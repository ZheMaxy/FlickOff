Touch Bar flickering: what to try on a MacBook Pro

A Touch Bar that flickers, flashes or blinks often does it in a dim room, when the strip lowers its own brightness. The rest of the Mac works fine. macOS has no brightness setting for the Touch Bar, and there's no confirmed fix, so here is what's worth trying, from the least to the most drastic.

First, check whether low brightness is the trigger

Wait until it flickers, then shine a light, such as your phone's, at the top of the screen near the camera, where the ambient light sensor is. One owner of a 13-inch MacBook Pro reports that it's worse in dim light and that light on the sensor stops it immediately.

If yours reacts the same way, the panel misbehaves at low brightness and is steady higher up, which is the case a fixed brightness can help with. If it flashes in bright light too, skip to getting it checked.

What to try

1. Update macOS and restart

It costs nothing, and it rules out a one-off glitch. Don't expect much if it has been flickering for weeks.

2. Restart the Touch Bar's service

This command stops the process that runs the Touch Bar, and macOS starts it again:

sudo pkill TouchBarServer

Owners use this as a short-term workaround, so expect the flicker to come back.

3. On an Intel model, reset the SMC and NVRAM

This is the usual advice in Apple's forums for Intel Macs, with mixed results. Apple silicon Macs don't have these resets to perform.

4. Keep the Touch Bar out of low brightness

This is what FlickOff is for, on Apple silicon. Choose Manual and a level high enough to stay steady, separately from the screen, and set When idle so it turns off instead of dimming into the range that flickers. It started with an M1 MacBook Pro that flickers at low brightness but not at a higher fixed level. Whether yours behaves the same, only trying it can tell.

5. Turn its display off

If no level is comfortable, and you can live without seeing it, you can turn off the Touch Bar's display and keep Touch ID.

6. Get it checked

A software workaround can't repair a failing panel. If it flashes at every brightness, or if it gets worse, Apple's repair options are the next step, and you can ask for a free diagnostic first. One owner reports that the flicker stopped after a battery service, so it's also worth checking whether yours is flagged for service in System Settings.

Questions

Is Touch Bar flickering a hardware problem?

Nobody outside Apple can say for certain, and it may not always have the same cause. That raising the brightness can stop it points to the panel behaving badly at low output, but it doesn't prove the panel is failing, or that it won't get worse.

Why can't I just lower or raise the Touch Bar's brightness in macOS?

macOS manages it automatically and offers no slider for it. The brightness controls on the Touch Bar adjust the main screen. That's the gap FlickOff fills.

Will a brighter Touch Bar burn in?

It's an OLED panel, so keeping it lit continuously raises the risk. Use the lowest level that stays steady, and let it turn off when idle.

Does FlickOff work on my Intel MacBook Pro?

No, it's for Apple silicon only. Steps 1 to 3 and 6 above still apply.
